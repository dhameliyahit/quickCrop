Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Before reaching out, check our quick FAQ to see if your query has already been answered:

  • Are my shipping label PDFs kept confidential?
    Yes! QuickCrop operates 100% inside your browser using client-side JavaScript. Zero files or personal customer data are ever uploaded to any server.
  • Which thermal printers are compatible?
    All standard 4x6" thermal direct barcode printers including TVS (LP 46 Lite, LP 46 Neo), TSC (DA210, DA220), Xprinter (XP-420B, XP-470B), Zebra, Gprinter, and Everycom.
  • Why are my barcodes blurry when printed?
    Blurry barcodes are caused when label tools convert vector text into low-DPI bitmap screenshots. QuickCrop preserves pure vector paths to ensure crisp, scan-ready prints. Read our Blurry Barcode Troubleshooting Guide.
