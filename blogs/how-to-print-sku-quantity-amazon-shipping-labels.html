How to Print SKU & Quantity on Amazon Shipping Labels (Avoid Wrong Parcel Packing)

Updated September 2026 • 6 min read • By QuickCrop Warehouse Operations Team

Why This Matters to Your Seller Rating:

Shipping the wrong item or wrong quantity to an Amazon customer triggers immediate negative feedback, Order Defect Rate (ODR) spikes, and costly return shipping charges. By printing the Seller SKU and quantity directly on your thermal shipping sticker, your order pickers can pack boxes 3x faster without cross-checking paper invoices.

The Missing Piece on Amazon Easy Ship Labels

When an order comes in on Amazon Seller Central, you schedule the dispatch and generate the shipping document. If you inspect the standard shipping label portion, you will notice something critical: there is no clear item name, SKU, or quantity listed on the sticker itself.

Amazon places all item details on the right half of the sheet inside the customer tax invoice. For sellers using thermal sticker printers, this creates a major logistical bottleneck:

  • If you only print the 4x6 shipping sticker, your warehouse picker has no idea whether the parcel contains a Blue T-Shirt (Size M) or a Black T-Shirt (Size XL).
  • To solve this, sellers often print hundreds of paper invoices on regular A4 printers just so their staff can see what item to pack.
  • This doubles your printing costs, wastes paper and toner, and slows down dispatch during festival sales like Great Indian Festival.

How QuickCrop Automates SKU & Quantity Stamping

Warehouse Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Here is how leading Amazon sellers streamline their packing line using QuickCrop:

⚡ Step-by-Step Packing SOP:

1. Download Batch PDF: Download 50–200 orders from Amazon Seller Central at once.

2. Upload to QuickCrop: Drop the PDF into QuickCrop. Ensure the "Print SKU & Qty on Thermal Label" checkbox is checked.

3. Print 4x6 Roll: Your thermal printer prints all 4x6 labels consecutively. Each label now clearly displays: SHIRT-BLUE-M | Qty - 1.

4. Batch Order Picking: Your warehouse staff picks the items directly matching the text on the roll. Packing speed increases from 40 parcels/hour to 120+ parcels/hour.

5. Separate Invoices: Click "Download Invoices Only (PDF)" in QuickCrop to save all customer invoices in a single clean PDF for your monthly GST accountant.
