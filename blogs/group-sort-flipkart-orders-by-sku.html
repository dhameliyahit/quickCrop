How to Group & Sort Flipkart Orders by SKU for 3x Faster Packing

Updated September 2026 • 4 min read • By Warehouse Operations Specialist

The Daily Struggle of Big Dispatch Batches

When you have 50, 100, or 500 orders to pack before the 2:00 PM courier pickup cutoff, packaging can become chaotic. In the standard Flipkart PDF, orders appear in random sequence:

  • Order 1 is a Blue T-Shirt (Size M).
  • Order 2 is a Wireless Mouse.
  • Order 3 is a Red T-Shirt (Size L).
  • Order 4 is a Blue T-Shirt (Size M) again.

Your packing staff ends up walking back and forth across warehouse shelves, searching for items one by one. Worse, tired workers often paste the wrong label onto the wrong parcel, leading to costly buyer returns (RTO) and seller account penalties.

How Batch SKU Sorting Works in QuickCrop

QuickCrop reads the text layer of your uploaded Flipkart PDF and automatically parses the SKU ID | Description line for each order.

When you select Sort by SKU (A-Z):

  1. All labels for the same SKU are grouped together consecutively in the output PDF.
  2. Your warehouse team can pick 30 units of "Wireless Mouse" in one trip.
  3. The picker places the items on the packing table and applies the first 30 labels in rapid succession without stopping to read descriptions!

The Result: 70% Less Packing Time

Sellers using SKU grouping report an average reduction in packing time from 45 minutes down to just 14 minutes per 50 orders, completely eliminating wrong item dispatch errors.
