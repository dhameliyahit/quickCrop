A4 Paper vs 4x6 Thermal Sticker: How Much Money Do Sellers Really Save?

Updated September 2026 • 5 min read • By Seller Finance & Operations Team

The Hidden Costs of Printing on A4 Paper

Many beginners start by printing Flipkart order invoices on ordinary 75 GSM A4 paper using an HP laser printer or an Epson inkjet printer. While the initial equipment is already lying in your office, the per-order costs quickly add up:

  • A4 Paper Cost: ₹0.50 per sheet.
  • Laser Toner Cartridge: ₹0.40 per printed page.
  • Clear Cello Packing Tape: ₹0.35 to ₹0.50 per parcel to cover and stick the label on the polybag.
  • Scissors Cutting Labor: 40–50 seconds per parcel just cutting and taping!

The Cost Comparison Table (Per 1,000 Orders)

Paper / Label Media ₹500 (1,000 A4 Sheets) ₹750 (2 rolls of 500 stickers)
Ink / Toner ₹400 (Toner refill/wear) ₹0 (Zero ink needed)

Total Material Cost ₹1,350 ₹750
Labor Packing Time 12.5 Hours 2.2 Hours (82% faster)

How Much Do You Save Annually?

A seller doing 30 orders daily (approx 10,000 orders/year) saves:

  • ₹6,000+ in raw material costs (no toner, no tape rolls).
  • Over 100 hours of manual labor, allowing you to dispatch higher volumes before courier cutoff time.
