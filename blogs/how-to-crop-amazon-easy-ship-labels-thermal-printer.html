How to Crop Amazon Easy Ship Labels for 4x6 Thermal Printers (Step-by-Step Guide)

Updated September 2026 • 7 min read • By QuickCrop Seller Logistics Team

Quick Summary:

Amazon Seller Central generates shipping labels on A4 sheets with 2 orders per page (shipping label on the left, tax invoice on the right). Printing this A4 sheet on a 4x6" thermal printer results in tiny, unreadable barcodes or massive paper waste. Using QuickCrop, you can split multi-order PDFs in 1-click, automatically stamp the product SKU & quantity onto the label, and print crisp, scan-ready 4x6 labels without uploading your confidential customer files to any server.

The Problem: Amazon Easy Ship A4 Sheets vs Thermal Printers

If you sell on Amazon India using Easy Ship, you already know the frustration: when you schedule dispatches and download your shipping labels, Amazon hands you a standard A4 PDF file (595 × 842 points). Each page is split down the middle:

  • Left side: The ATSPL shipping label containing the delivery address, routing code, customer barcode, and package dimensions.
  • Right side: The customer tax invoice and seller declaration.

Furthermore, Amazon packs two complete orders on each A4 sheet (one in the top half and one in the bottom half). If you try printing this PDF directly to your 4x6 thermal printer (such as a TSC DA210, TVS LP 46, Xprinter XP-420B, or Zebra ZD220), the printer driver scales the entire A4 sheet down. The result? A minuscule, blurry 2×3" sticker where the Code128 barcode lines bleed together. When the Amazon ATSPL pickup associate arrives with their handheld terminal, the scanner beeps an error or fails completely.

Why Cutting Labels with Scissors or Photoshop is Costing You Money

Many sellers resort to painful workarounds:

  1. Using the Windows Snipping Tool: Taking screenshots of each label degrades vector lines into 72 DPI raster pixels. Barcodes become fuzzy and unreadable.
  2. Manual PDF cropping in Photoshop or Acrobat: Cropping 50 to 200 orders manually takes 45–60 minutes every single morning.
  3. Printing full A4 sheets on laser paper: Buying A4 paper, replacement toner cartridges, and wide transparent cello tape costs roughly ₹2.80 to ₹3.50 per parcel compared to ₹0.65 for a direct thermal sticker roll.

The Free Solution: 1-Click Vector Cropping with QuickCrop

QuickCrop's Amazon Easy Ship Label Crop Tool was built specifically to solve this dilemma for Indian marketplace sellers. Here is the step-by-step workflow:

Step 1: Download Your Orders PDF from Amazon Seller Central

Log in to Amazon Seller Central India. Navigate to Orders > Manage Orders. Filter by "Unshipped" and schedule your Easy Ship orders for pickup. Click Print Shipping Labels to download your combined PDF file.

Step 2: Drop the PDF into QuickCrop

Open QuickCrop Amazon Easy Ship Cropper in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, or Safari. Drag your downloaded PDF and drop it into the upload box.

Within 2 seconds, QuickCrop's client-side vector engine inspects each page coordinate stream:

  • It identifies whether the page contains 1 order or 2 orders.
  • It extracts the left-side thermal label boundaries with exact mathematical precision (excluding the dashed scissor lines).
  • It centers the label inside a standard 4 × 6 inch (288 × 432 pt) canvas.
  • It reads the Seller SKU and quantity from the tax invoice and stamps it directly into the empty space on the label.
⚡ Pro-Tip: Zero Server Uploads

Unlike other free conversion websites that upload your seller PDFs to remote cloud servers (exposing customer names, addresses, and phone numbers), QuickCrop processes 100% of your data inside your web browser. Your customer data never leaves your computer.

Step 3: Keep the SKU & Quantity Stamp Enabled

One of the biggest pain points in warehouse packing is matching packages to orders. Usually, sellers must print the tax invoice separately just to know what item goes inside the box. QuickCrop automatically reads the SKU (e.g. BLUE-SHIRT-M | Qty - 1) and stamps it directly onto the sticker. Your packing team can pack the parcel straight from the thermal label without wasting paper!

Step 4: Configure Your Thermal Printer Driver

Before printing your first batch, verify your thermal printer driver settings in Windows:

  • Page Size: Select 100mm × 150mm (or 4.00 × 6.00 in).
  • Scale: In the Chrome/Edge print dialog, choose Actual Size (100%). Never choose "Fit to printable area".
  • Margins: Set to None.
  • Print Darkness / Density: In printer preferences, set darkness to 8 or 10. Avoid maximum darkness, which causes thermal ink bleeding on barcode gaps.
