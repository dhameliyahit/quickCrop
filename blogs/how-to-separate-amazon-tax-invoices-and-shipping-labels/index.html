How to Separate Tax Invoices from Amazon Shipping Labels for GST Accounting

Updated September 2026 • 6 min read • By QuickCrop E-Commerce Accounting Team

GST Compliance Tip:

Under Indian GST laws (Section 36 of CGST Act), businesses must maintain sales invoices and books of accounts for 72 months (6 years). Thermal sticker rolls fade within a few months. Separating your digital tax invoices into PDF archives ensures effortless GSTR-1 reconciliation and long-term tax compliance.

Why Amazon Bundles Labels and Invoices Together

When Amazon launched Easy Ship in India, it combined the shipping label and the customer invoice into a single A4 PDF file. While this made sense for sellers using standard desktop laser printers and A4 sheets, it creates two major problems for modern e-commerce sellers using 4x6" direct thermal printers:

  1. Thermal Paper Waste: If you accidentally print both sides on thermal paper, you spend valuable sticker rolls on invoices that end up in the trash or inside package envelopes.
  2. The Fading Problem: Direct thermal paper works via heat-activated chemical coating. Over 3 to 6 months, exposure to ambient heat, light, and humidity turns thermal receipts pale and illegible. You cannot rely on physical thermal paper for GST tax audits.

Benefits for Your Monthly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B Filing

By keeping your invoices in a separate digital PDF:

  • Organized Folders: You can save monthly files like Amazon_Invoices_September_2026.pdf directly to Google Drive or your local accounting folder.
  • Easy Sharing with CA: Send the invoices PDF directly to your Chartered Accountant or tax consultant for GSTR-1 B2C sales reconciliation.
  • Audit Ready: If GST authorities request verification of invoice serial numbers or HSN codes, you have clean digital records available on demand.
