Frequently Asked Questions

What makes QuickLabelCrop the best tool for Flipkart label crop?
QuickLabelCrop is built specifically for Indian online sellers. It provides 100% client-side privacy (no files ever uploaded), zero loss in vector barcode sharpness, instant 1-click cropping for multi-order PDFs, automated SKU sorting for fast order picking, and 1-click separate invoice extraction for GST filing.
Is QuickLabelCrop completely free?
Yes, QuickLabelCrop (QuickCrop) is 100% free forever with unlimited crops. There are no subscriptions, no per-label fees, no watermarks, and no registration required.
How do I install QuickLabelCrop as a Web App on PC or Mobile?
Simply click the "Install App" button in our navigation header or the "Install Web App" button in the hero. On Chrome/Edge, click the install icon (⊕) in your address bar. On Android, tap the three dots and select "Install app". On iPhone/iPad, tap Share and select "Add to Home Screen".
Will EKart and Delhivery barcode scanners scan cropped labels?
Yes, 100%! QuickLabelCrop extracts original vector coordinate boundaries rather than taking low-resolution screenshots. Courier pickup agents will be able to scan your barcodes and QR codes instantly without scan errors.
Where is the dedicated Flipkart tool page located?
You can access the dedicated Flipkart shipping label cropper directly at /flipkart-shipping-label-crop-tool/ with specialized thermal printer settings.
Can I separate invoices from shipping labels?
Yes! QuickLabelCrop provides an instant button to download separate invoices into their own clean PDF file for your monthly GST accounting while printing your 4x6 labels.
