How to Crop Flipkart Shipping Labels (Step-by-Step)

QuickCrop automates your dispatch workflow in 3 effortless steps:

  1. Download Orders: In your Flipkart Seller Hub dashboard, navigate to Orders > Active Orders and click "Generate Labels" to download your order PDF.
  2. Upload to QuickCrop: Click "Upload Flipkart Label File" above or drag and drop the PDF (or label image) into the box.
  3. Download & Print: QuickCrop extracts only the shipping label box and discards the tax invoice. Click "Download Cropped Shipping Labels" to print on your 4x6" thermal printer!

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Flipkart shipping label crop tool free to use?
Yes, 100% free forever! You don't need any subscription, credit card, or account registration to crop your Flipkart seller order labels.
Are customer addresses and invoice details secure?
Yes, completely safe! All cropping is processed client-side inside your browser using JavaScript. No files, customer addresses, or order details are ever sent to any remote server.
Can I crop multiple Flipkart orders at once?
Yes! Our tool supports bulk multi-page Flipkart PDF files. Whether you have 2 orders or 200 orders, it crops the whole batch in under a second.
Can I crop Flipkart label photos or screenshots?
Yes, you can drop any image (.png, .jpg, .jpeg, .webp) of your Flipkart shipping label or invoice sheet. QuickCrop will auto-crop the label box and let you download it as a thermal PDF or PNG.
How does SKU sorting help during order preparation?
When you select "Group & Sort by SKU", QuickCrop rearranges all orders so identical products are printed consecutively. Your warehouse team can pick items in bulk and pack them without repeatedly switching between different products.
