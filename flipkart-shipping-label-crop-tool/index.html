Thermal Barcode Printer Setup for Flipkart Labels

Safe, standard printing configuration for Indian e-commerce sellers using 4×6 thermal printers (TSC, TVS, Xprinter, Zebra):

Printer Compatibility Standard Media Size Margins Scaling & Layout Barcode Calibration
TSC TE244 / TE200 100mm × 150mm (4×6") 0 mm (Zero Margins) 100% / Actual Size Calibrate per manufacturer guide; preserve quiet zones
TVS LP 46 Neo 100mm × 150mm (4×6") 0 mm (Zero Margins) 100% / Actual Size Calibrate per manufacturer guide; preserve quiet zones
Xprinter XP-420B 100mm × 150mm (4×6") 0 mm (Zero Margins) 100% / Actual Size Calibrate per manufacturer guide; preserve quiet zones
Zebra ZD220 / ZD230 100mm × 150mm (4×6") 0 mm (Zero Margins) 100% / Actual Size Calibrate per manufacturer guide; preserve quiet zones

Safe Printing Checklist:

  • Media Size: Always set your thermal roll label size to 100×150 mm (4×6 inches) in your printer driver preferences.
  • Scale: Select "Actual Size" (100%) in your print dialog. Avoid "Fit to Printable Area", which distorts barcode line ratios.
  • Calibration: Run the manufacturer's auto-gap sensor calibration whenever loading a fresh roll of labels.
  • Quiet Zones: Ensure there is clean white space on both the left and right sides of the barcode to help EKart and Delhivery scanners read the parcel smoothly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes QuickCrop the best Flipkart shipping label crop tool?

QuickCrop is completely free and operates 100% inside your browser. No files or order details are uploaded to remote servers. It preserves original vector barcode paths to help courier pickup agents scan labels reliably, and it includes automated SKU sorting for faster packing.

Is this Flipkart shipping label crop tool free to use?

Yes, 100% free forever! You don't need any subscription, credit card, or account registration to crop your Flipkart seller order labels.

How do I install QuickCrop as an app on my PC or phone?

Click the "Install App" button in our navigation bar or the "Install Web App" badge in the tool header. You can also click the (⊕) icon in the Chrome/Edge address bar to install it as a lightweight desktop app.

Are customer addresses and invoice details secure?

Yes, completely safe! All cropping is processed client-side inside your browser using JavaScript. No files, customer addresses, or order details are ever sent to any remote server.

Can I do bulk Flipkart shipping label crop from a multi-order PDF?

Yes! QuickCrop is specifically designed for bulk Flipkart shipping label crop tasks. Whether you have 2 orders or 200 orders, it crops the whole batch in under a second while preserving pure vector barcode clarity.

How do I crop and print Flipkart shipping labels for a 4x6 thermal printer? (Flipkart label kaise print kare?)

Upload your official Flipkart order PDF into QuickCrop. The tool isolates the shipping label, removes the tax invoice, and formats it for standard 4x6 inch (100x150 mm) thermal rolls. In your print dialog, select your thermal printer (TSC, TVS, Zebra, or Xprinter) and choose "Actual Size (100%)" to help courier pickup scanners read barcodes reliably.

Can I crop Flipkart label photos or screenshots?

Yes, you can drop any image (.png, .jpg, .jpeg, .webp) of your Flipkart shipping label or invoice sheet. QuickCrop will auto-crop the label box and let you download it as a thermal PDF or PNG.

How does SKU sorting help during order preparation?

When multi-order PDFs are processed, QuickCrop automatically sorts orders by SKU so identical products are printed consecutively. Your warehouse team can pick items in bulk and pack them without repeatedly switching between different products.

Why do Flipkart labels print blurred or unreadable on some thermal printers?

Many online converters turn PDF pages into low-resolution bitmap images, which softens the sharp edges of 1D Code128 barcodes. QuickCrop solves this by performing pure vector coordinate adjustments directly on the PDF without rasterizing, preserving original vector barcode lines designed for reliable courier scanning.

What printer paper size should I select in Windows for Flipkart labels?

In your printer driver preferences, set paper size to 100mm × 150mm (or 4.00 × 6.00 inches) with 0 mm margins. When printing from Chrome or Adobe Acrobat, always choose 'Actual Size (100%)' and avoid 'Fit to Page' to prevent distortion.
