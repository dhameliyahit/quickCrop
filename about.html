About QuickCrop

Empowering online sellers with fast, private, and effortless shipping label preparation.

Our Mission

QuickCrop was created to solve one of the most frustrating bottlenecks faced by Indian e-commerce sellers every day: preparing PDF shipping labels from online marketplaces (such as Flipkart and Amazon Easy Ship) for standard 4x6" thermal sticker printers.

Most marketplace seller portals bundle shipping labels, buyer invoices, and return manifests onto large A4 pages. Manually cropping, taking screenshots, or rotating these documents wastes hours of valuable dispatch time and often causes blurry barcodes that courier pickup agents fail to scan. QuickCrop eliminates this hassle with instant 1-click batch label extraction.

🔒 Why 100% Client-Side Privacy Matters

Unlike other online conversion websites that upload your confidential seller invoices and customer personal information (names, phone numbers, addresses, product values) to remote cloud servers, QuickCrop processes 100% of your PDF files inside your browser using WebAssembly & JavaScript. Your files never touch our servers or any third-party storage.

Key Innovations Built Into QuickCrop

  • Vector Barcode Precision: We preserve original vector coordinates directly inside the PDF stream so your 1D & 2D barcodes remain razor-sharp on TSC, TVS, Xprinter, Zebra, and Gprinter machines.
  • Automatic Invoice Separation: With a single click, separate GST tax invoices into their own clean archive PDF while formatting the 4x6 sticker labels for dispatch.
  • Intelligent SKU Batching: Automatically sorts and prints labels grouped by SKU so warehouse staff can pack identical items faster with fewer dispatch mistakes.
  • Progressive Web App (PWA): Install QuickCrop directly onto your Windows PC, Mac, Android, or iOS device to use it seamlessly both online and offline.

100% Free For All Sellers

QuickCrop is committed to remaining 100% free forever. There are no subscriptions, per-label limits, hidden paywalls, or mandatory logins. We support the maintenance and development of QuickCrop through unobtrusive contextual advertisements (via Google AdSense).
