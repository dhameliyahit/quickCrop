Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

At QuickCrop (accessible from https://www.quickcrop.online/), your privacy and the confidentiality of your customer order data is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is handled, and how we protect your rights under applicable privacy regulations.

🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy Commitment:

When you use QuickCrop to crop shipping labels from Flipkart, Amazon Easy Ship, or Meesho, all processing happens directly inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Your PDF files, customer names, delivery addresses, contact numbers, order items, and tax invoice amounts are NEVER uploaded to any external server or cloud database.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike traditional cloud PDF conversion tools, QuickCrop does not require you to create an account, log in, or transmit document files over the internet.

  • No Document Uploads: Your shipping label PDFs and invoice sheets remain strictly within your device's memory.
  • No Buyer PII Storage: We do not store, log, read, sell, or monetize customer personally identifiable information (PII).
  • No Registration Required: QuickCrop is 100% free with zero registration or sign-up needed.

2. Information Automatically Collected (Log Files & Analytics)

Like most modern websites, QuickCrop collects standard technical metrics that browsers automatically transmit when requesting web pages. This non-personal data includes:

  • Browser type and version (e.g. Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
  • Device operating system (e.g. Windows, macOS, Android, iOS).
  • Referring website addresses and exit pages.
  • Date and timestamp of visits and pages viewed.

This data is aggregated using Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics (GA4) solely to optimize server performance, diagnose software errors, and understand which marketplace tools sellers use most frequently.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

QuickCrop uses Google AdSense to serve contextual advertisements across our website to keep our shipping label cropping tools 100% free for Indian e-commerce sellers.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on QuickCrop.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to QuickCrop and/or other websites on the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Progressive Web App (PWA) & Offline Cache

QuickCrop uses modern browser Service Workers and Cache Storage APIs to enable offline functionality and lightning-fast loading speeds. These cached assets include application layout stylesheets, icons, and JavaScript logic files. No user PDF data is ever permanently written to your device's storage without your explicit action (such as clicking "Download Cropped Labels").

5. Compliance with Privacy Regulations

We adhere to the principles of data minimization and privacy by design in compliance with international and regional privacy standards, including:

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), India: Ensuring complete protection of Indian seller and consumer data through zero-collection architecture.
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): Protecting visitors from the European Economic Area with transparent cookie policies and zero tracking of private files.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA): We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
